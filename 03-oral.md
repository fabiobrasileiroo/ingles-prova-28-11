# Prova oral — roteiro de treino

**Como treinar:** leia a pergunta, responda **em voz alta** (grave no celular), 3-4 frases. Depois ouça e confira: usei o tempo verbal certo? Frases curtas e certas valem mais que frases longas com erro.
**Fórmula de resposta (A-R-E):** **A**nswer (responde direto) → **R**eason (because…) → **E**xample/extra (uma frase a mais).

> *"Have you ever eaten tacacá?"* → *"Yes, I have. I've eaten it many times **because** it's very popular in Manaus. I **love** it, but it's really spicy!"*

## Unidade 5 — opinião e conselho
1. Which do you prefer, reading books or watching movies? Why?
2. What kind of music do you like? What kind can't you stand?
3. What's your favorite movie / series / game genre? Recommend one to me.
4. I want to improve my English. What should I do? (dê 3 conselhos: should / could / need to)
5. What TV shows should everybody watch? Why?

## Unidade 6 — invenções
1. What is the most important invention for you? When was it invented? Who was it invented by?
2. What is your cellphone made of?
3. Compare an old invention and a new one (use opposites: heavy × light, slow × fast…).
4. Do you think the internet was a good invention? Why? (*I think so / I don't think so*)

## Unidade 7 — experiências e comida ⭐
1. Have you ever eaten a very unusual regional food? How did it taste?
2. What is something you have never done but would love to do?
3. How long have you lived in Manaus? (for / since)
4. Have you ever travelled to another state/country?
5. What have you bought at the supermarket this week? (countable/uncountable + measure words)
6. Tell me a dish you know how to prepare.

Vocabulário de sabor: *sweet, sour, salty, bitter, spicy, greasy, delicious, tasty, disgusting*.

## Unidade 8 — estudo
1. What are the pros and cons of online classes?
2. What happens if you procrastinate? (zero conditional)
3. What do you do when you feel overwhelmed?
4. Are you a freshman, a sophomore, a junior or a senior? What's your course?

## Possível pergunta do quadro (foto 1 — usa *will*, revisão da U3)
Se o professor usar estas perguntas na oral:
- *Which country will you travel to?* · *What will you do there?* · *Where will you stay?* · *How long will you stay in this country?*
→ Responder com **will + verbo puro**: *I will travel to Japan. I will visit Tokyo and eat ramen. I'll stay in a hostel. I'll stay there for two weeks.*

## Tarefas para entregar (confirme com o professor)

**1. Writing 9 — Unidade 6 (página 70 do livro):** invenção da sua casa.
Estrutura (escreva com SUAS palavras, 6-8 linhas):
1. *The invention I chose is the ___.* (apresenta)
2. *It was invented by ___ in ___.* (passiva + ano)
3. *It is made of ___ and ___.* (materiais)
4. *Old ___ were ___, but modern ones are ___.* (opostos de adjetivos)
5. *I have used it ___ .* (present perfect, bônus)
6. *I think it is ___ because ___.* (opinião)
Candidatos fáceis de pesquisar: micro-ondas, geladeira, ventilador, liquidificador, máquina de lavar, lâmpada.

**2. Homework da Unidade 7 (slide do prof):** *"Write a text about something that you have never done but you would love to do."*
1. *I have never ___ .* 2. *I have always wanted to ___ because ___.* 3. *I have already ___ (algo parecido).* 4. *I would love to ___ with ___ .*
